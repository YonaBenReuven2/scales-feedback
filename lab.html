Tests the piano filter on your piano, mic and room. The piano plays a calibration while you stay quiet, then a short exercise to sing along to, with the piano playing your notes. Afterwards you can listen to what the filter left behind: it should sound like just your voice.

Inputs

The piano (MIDI)Not connected
Opens the mic, the piano’s audio and the piano itself.

Test

About 40 seconds. Sing as you normally would, without headphones.

What the filter did

–Delay from the piano’s audio to the mic
–Piano removed while you were quiet
